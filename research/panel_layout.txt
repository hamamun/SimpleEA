SimpleAdvisor - what the panel will look like
=============================================

This is the output of walk_trade_plan.py: it lays the panel out on a
character grid where one character is one pixel-column of the real font,
so what you see below is where the text actually lands.

  Group 1 (top)   : the readings - unchanged from the current version.
  the line        : ONE separator line only.  There is no 'Trade plan'
                    heading.
  Group 2 (bottom): one plan per timeframe.  A HOLD or loading timeframe
                    takes a single line, so the group is only as tall as
                    the signals it actually has, and the box is only as
                    wide as its longest line.

The last section sweeps the terminal's DPI (72..192) and the font size
(6..16) to check the width budget the EA uses for its characters.
