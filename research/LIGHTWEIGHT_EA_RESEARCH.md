# Lightweight MT5 EA: source review and proposed design

**Review date:** 29 October 2026. **Status:** research and design only; no new EA has been implemented, compiled, tested in MetaTrader, or traded. The following source findings describe the pinned versions, not every version of each project. A code path is not proof that it caused a particular live result.

**Update:** The later, self-contained [Automate plan](../automate.md) supersedes this report's initial design suggestions and earlier questions. The user confirmed one position per timeframe per symbol, with M5, M15 and M30 positions allowed simultaneously; all lanes and automatic trading start enabled. One main TP and one broker SL are wanted, with no partial targets or trailing in version 1. Broker, account and symbol details should be discovered at runtime. A 1%-equity default and percentage-bypass options are recommendations in the later plan, not a user-specified risk percentage.

## 1. Executive comparison

| Project | Most useful idea | Main issue found | What to carry forward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Predict_X** | Aladin remembers similar setups and reports their outcomes. | Retrospective rehearsal uses current AI weights; some trade requests are marked successful without verifying the server result; lot sizing can fall back to the minimum lot. | Remember and compare setups, but keep projected and real outcomes separate and initially use memory for **observation only**. |
| **Autotrader** | Scores BUY and SELL separately, with a useful decision/audit trail. | Its market plan and lot size are based on the opposite quote side from the market entry; several sequential gates may also suppress entries. | Visible evidence and explanations, but correct execution-side prices and test each non-safety gate independently. |
| **Emtt** | Volatility-clustered Supertrend and bounded, transparent learning. | The real-trade net calculation omits `DEAL_FEE`; learning combines real and paper labels, while several small-sample adaptations can overfit. | Use regime context and record the **full attributable net result**, not a live adaptive model in version 1. |
| **SimpleEA** | Separate directional signals from permission to open a trade, with M5/M15/M30 rows and structure-based plans. | Several valid-looking signals can fail separate plan gates; the replay's pending-fill assumption differs from the live order lifetime; target quantiles can be misunderstood. | Preserve the familiar panel and signal/trade-HOLD distinction, but simplify the plan and make each reason visible. |

## 2. Evidence and limitations by project

### Predict_X — pinned `65151d15fd6766fe3d503d430c039548675a905a`

- **Useful:** the [Aladin memory bank](https://github.com/hamamun/Predict_X/blob/65151d15fd6766fe3d503d430c039548675a905a/Include/PXM_Book.mqh#L1-L16) stores historical and live setups and uses up to 50 nearest neighbors, with a 30-sample readiness floor. It can refuse, resize or modify a plan. The general idea of asking “what happened in comparable situations?” is valuable, provided the data and execution assumptions are honest.
- **Confirmed replay limitation:** [rehearsal](https://github.com/hamamun/Predict_X/blob/65151d15fd6766fe3d503d430c039548675a905a/Include/PXM_Rehearse.mqh#L730-L748) intentionally scores past bars with the **current** online-AI weights. It also substitutes typical spread conditions. These are reconstructed present-model scenarios, not necessarily the predictions or fills available at those historical times. The weights can contain information acquired later than the old bar.
- **Confirmed state/label limitations:** the [online-AI key](https://github.com/hamamun/Predict_X/blob/65151d15fd6766fe3d503d430c039548675a905a/Include/PX_OnlineAI.mqh#L44-L47) contains the symbol but not the timeframe, so chart instances can overwrite the same stored weights. Its [learning label](https://github.com/hamamun/Predict_X/blob/65151d15fd6766fe3d503d430c039548675a905a/Include/PX_OnlineAI.mqh#L159-L173) is future close direction, not the realized net result of the order. A displayed AI percentage therefore is not demonstrated to be a calibrated probability of a profitable trade.
- **Confirmed execution risks:** [sizing](https://github.com/hamamun/Predict_X/blob/65151d15fd6766fe3d503d430c039548675a905a/Include/PX_SignalLifecycle.mqh#L65-L87) can return or clamp to minimum volume even when that exceeds the intended risk amount. Its volume-digit method also needs validation for non-decimal steps such as 0.25. The [trade manager](https://github.com/hamamun/Predict_X/blob/65151d15fd6766fe3d503d430c039548675a905a/Include/PX_TradeManager.mqh#L455-L504) can convert a too-close limit to a market request without rebuilding its plan and accepts the `CTrade` boolean as success without checking the server retcode. The official reference says that `Buy()` returning true alone does **not** establish execution [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/standardlibrary/tradeclasses/ctrade/ctradebuy).

### Autotrader — pinned `2f5014ae1ffad6eea802377c8a86b7323630c46a`

- **Useful:** the [direction engine](https://github.com/hamamun/Autotrader/blob/2f5014ae1ffad6eea802377c8a86b7323630c46a/MQL5/Include/Autotrader/DirectionEngine.mqh#L801-L868) evaluates both directions and exposes components; the trade and safety log records decisions and failures.
- **Confirmed quote-side mismatch:** [planning](https://github.com/hamamun/Autotrader/blob/2f5014ae1ffad6eea802377c8a86b7323630c46a/MQL5/Include/Autotrader/DirectionEngine.mqh#L901-L910) starts a market BUY plan at Bid and a SELL plan at Ask. [Execution and sizing](https://github.com/hamamun/Autotrader/blob/2f5014ae1ffad6eea802377c8a86b7323630c46a/MQL5/Include/Autotrader/TradeEngine.mqh#L861-L865) use that plan, but market BUY opens at Ask and SELL at Bid [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/trading). Example for EURUSD: Bid `1.10000`, Ask `1.10010`, planned BUY stop `1.09900`. The plan sizes for **10 pips** to stop; an actual fill at that Ask is **11 pips** from the stop, before slippage. Its reward distance is also reduced. This is a concrete source-level mechanism, not a measurement of its live losses.
- **Confirmed safety limitations:** volume rounds to nearest and the minimum lot is explicitly allowed to risk up to **three times** the configured amount; the margin calculation uses BUY even for sells and can proceed after a calculation failure ([source](https://github.com/hamamun/Autotrader/blob/2f5014ae1ffad6eea802377c8a86b7323630c46a/MQL5/Include/Autotrader/TradeEngine.mqh#L305-L362)). The market protective-level precheck also references Ask for BUY and Bid for SELL, unlike the long-Bid/short-Ask position-side checks in the official example [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/structures/mqltraderequest); broker behavior must be tested. Auto trading defaults ON while the initial broker SL defaults OFF ([inputs](https://github.com/hamamun/Autotrader/blob/2f5014ae1ffad6eea802377c8a86b7323630c46a/MQL5/Experts/Autotrader/Autotrader.mq5#L27-L30)).
- **Plausible, unmeasured strictness:** regime, M30 direction, spread, target geometry and a high score threshold are applied sequentially ([gates](https://github.com/hamamun/Autotrader/blob/2f5014ae1ffad6eea802377c8a86b7323630c46a/MQL5/Include/Autotrader/DirectionEngine.mqh#L871-L894), [final checks](https://github.com/hamamun/Autotrader/blob/2f5014ae1ffad6eea802377c8a86b7323630c46a/MQL5/Include/Autotrader/DirectionEngine.mqh#L950-L998)). They can reduce trading frequency, but this review did not measure which gate blocked how often or whether removing a gate improves performance.

### Emtt — pinned `01344ece91b30e679108df18dd53584cff871f47`

- **Useful:** the [Supertrend module](https://github.com/hamamun/Emtt/blob/01344ece91b30e679108df18dd53584cff871f47/Include/Emtt/Emtt_Supertrend.mqh#L17-L39) groups volatility into three clusters and bounds the parameters it considers. The system also scores trend, structure, flow and higher-timeframe context. The idea of treating market conditions as context is useful even without copying its ATR-based bands or adaptive machinery.
- **Confirmed accounting omission:** [history net](https://github.com/hamamun/Emtt/blob/01344ece91b30e679108df18dd53584cff871f47/Include/Emtt/Emtt_TradeManager.mqh#L455-L492) adds deal profit, swap and commission but not `DEAL_FEE`. If a broker posts a separate fee on those deals, the recorded result can be too optimistic and can even change the win/loss label. MT5 defines `DEAL_FEE` separately from commission [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/tradingconstants/dealproperties).
- **Plausible model risk:** [learning](https://github.com/hamamun/Emtt/blob/01344ece91b30e679108df18dd53584cff871f47/Include/Emtt/Emtt_Learning.mqh#L195-L245) mixes projected target/stop labels for paper ideas with realized net-point signs for real trades. Its bounded adjustment starts after 30 labels; the [threshold optimizer](https://github.com/hamamun/Emtt/blob/01344ece91b30e679108df18dd53584cff871f47/Include/Emtt/Emtt_ParamOpt.mqh#L14-L22) can change a bar after 20 accepted and 20 near-miss observations. Small, heterogeneous samples and several adaptive layers can be unstable; actual overfitting was not measured here.
- **Default safety:** Auto Trading is OFF, but if it is enabled without changing other inputs, the separate initial broker SL and free-margin check remain OFF ([inputs](https://github.com/hamamun/Emtt/blob/01344ece91b30e679108df18dd53584cff871f47/Experts/Emtt.mq5#L44-L53)). An internal planned stop is not protection held at the broker.

### SimpleEA — pinned `1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc`

- **Useful:** separate **signal** from **trade permission**. For example, `BUY | Steady | HOLD: spread` means the direction is still BUY, but a new order cannot be placed. A signal HOLD instead means no actionable direction has been declared. Current SimpleEA can show an existing directional score while a Choppy/Mixed permission blocks only new entries ([source](https://github.com/hamamun/SimpleEA/blob/1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc/SimpleAdvisor.mq5#L1345-L1367)). `Steady` does not bypass risk, broker, target or account checks.
- **Confirmed possible HOLD causes:** the current planner requires a retail **hedging** account, usable Bid bars, valid structure, at least 30 matching observations, ordered TP1/TP2/TP3 on the tick grid, and the main TP3 to clear the extra spread screen ([planner](https://github.com/hamamun/SimpleEA/blob/1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc/SimpleAdvisor.mq5#L2320-L2376), [targets](https://github.com/hamamun/SimpleEA/blob/1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc/SimpleAdvisor.mq5#L2414-L2469)). These can prevent a Steady signal from placing an order. Whether any particular gate explains the earlier screenshot is unknown without its broker/account details and logs. On a netting account, MT5 has one net position per symbol, so the current EA deliberately holds instead of isolating its three lanes [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/book/automation/account/account_netting_hedge).
- **Important target interpretation:** raw TP1, TP2 and TP3 are the 50th, 70th and 85th percentiles of past favorable excursions **in units of each past stop risk**, multiplied by today's stop distance, then possibly capped by current opposing swings. They are **not** 50%, 70% and 85% probabilities of reaching those targets, and TP1/TP2 are not currently percentages of TP3. With 100 distinct ordered observations, the code's 85th-percentile index has 16/100 observations at or above it; with 30, it has 6/30. Ties and today's swing cap change this illustrative count. It says nothing reliable about a future trade's win probability or net expectancy. See [quantiles and target construction](https://github.com/hamamun/SimpleEA/blob/1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc/SimpleAdvisor.mq5#L2176-L2182) and [plan prices](https://github.com/hamamun/SimpleEA/blob/1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc/SimpleAdvisor.mq5#L2432-L2446).
- **Confirmed replay/live mismatch:** a simulated pending order counts a touch only on the **first following bar**, ignores that fill bar's favorable extreme, and resolves a stop first when intrabar ordering is unknown ([replay](https://github.com/hamamun/SimpleEA/blob/1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc/SimpleAdvisor.mq5#L2027-L2059)). Actual waiting orders may live for three trend bars or eight range bars and may be refreshed ([live manager](https://github.com/hamamun/SimpleEA/blob/1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc/SimpleAdvisor.mq5#L6431-L6464)). These are deliberately conservative approximations, but their distributions need not match live outcomes; touches are not proven fills. The 4/6/8-bar M5/M15/M30 observation horizons also do not guarantee progressively larger targets in price.
- **Signal and regime caveats:** the trend and its rate of change are related measurements, not statistically independent confirmations; a `3/3` score is not a probability. The Hurst estimator returns `0.5` when there is insufficient data or an unusable estimate, which can appear as Mixed rather than an explicit unknown ([estimator](https://github.com/hamamun/SimpleEA/blob/1385785629a8ac3c471e8ca2da8517ad580437bc/SimpleAdvisor.mq5#L1025-L1034)). Neither observation proves that the signal is wrong or that another estimator will be more accurate.
- **Cost caveat:** `TP3 > spread` is the requested **extra** screening rule, not an additional spread charged to the trade and not a net-profit guarantee. Commission, fees and slippage can still overwhelm a short partial target.

## 3. What current research suggests

1. A 2026 EUR/USD and USD/HKD high-frequency preprint reports that stronger technical/order-book signals ceased to be profitable after transaction costs. It is not a test of these EAs, but it illustrates why M5 signal accuracy alone is insufficient [1](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7314737).
2. A 2026 equity study using rolling HMM regimes reports that the regime feature helped some instruments and hurt others; its deflated Sharpe did not reach formal significance after multiple-testing correction. This is not evidence of an FX-scalping edge [1](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/15/6/1334). A September 2026 MQL5 example explores duration-aware regimes, but its limited XAUUSD M5 test calls for further validation [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/24460). **Start with an explainable regime, not an unvalidated HMM/HSMM.**
3. A September 2026 MQL5 competing-risks exit example treats TP, SL and the remaining open state separately; its exit model helped two tested instruments and hurt two others. Better probability modeling did not consistently improve trading results [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/24106). For our research, record **which happened first**, not just maximum favorable movement.
4. A 2026 filter study distinguishes selecting better trades from merely changing exposure and trade count; the example did not establish its filter's selection edge [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/23665). Count and test each discretionary gate rather than silently adding another one.
5. MT5 supports testing on broker real ticks, simulated request delay and a separate forward period. Pending-order delay is applied to placement, not server-side triggering [1](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/algotrading/testing). These tools improve the experiment but do not reproduce every live fill.

## 4. Proposed lightweight EA — a draft for agreement

**Goal:** a separate, small native MQL5 project, not another layer added to SimpleEA. Preserve the M5/M15/M30 interface and a broker-side initial stop; use **no ATR-multiplier formula for entry, TP or SL**. Favor an auditable baseline over an untested promise of higher accuracy. The following values and rules are **candidate starting points, not calibrated defaults**.

### A. Data, trend and signal

- Calculate once per newly **closed** bar for each timeframe; use only completed higher-timeframe data. Show `DATA WAIT` rather than inventing a regime or signal when required history is missing. Cache results; do only order management and fresh-quote checks on ticks.
- Start from SimpleEA's Ehlers Precision Trend and its rate of change, plus confirmed swing/net-move structure. One transparent candidate is a three-vote score: trend sign, trend-ROC sign with a tick-based deadband, and structure direction. `+2/+3` is BUY, `-2/-3` is SELL; otherwise signal HOLD. The two filter-derived votes are correlated; `2/3` is a score, not a 67% win chance. The deadband and agreement rule require broker-data testing.
- Show the trend and each contributing vote. Do not introduce multiple additional indicators whose only effect is to repeat the same momentum judgment. Higher-timeframe direction can be displayed for context rather than silently enforced.

### B. Regime

- Use the 20-closed-bar efficiency ratio, `ER = |close[t] - close[t-20]| / sum(|close[k] - close[k-1]|)`, alongside a simple recent-range/quote-shock flag. As an **untested illustration**, ER at least `0.30` could label Steady, ER at most `0.15` Choppy, and the middle Mixed; use a short two-bar confirmation for ordinary label changes, while a quote/shock safety condition is immediate.
- These labels would describe a different measurement from the current Hurst labels; they are not equivalent or proven superior. Show the numeric ER, label age and reason. Regime by itself does not erase BUY/SELL. Propose that Choppy/Mixed do not automatically block a valid direction unless that policy is explicitly chosen and validated. A Steady label permits an entry **when its plan and safety checks pass**.

### C. Entry and prices

- **Recommended entry policy:** keep SimpleEA's familiar strong `3/3` market versus `2/3` pullback-limit distinction. A market order uses a fresh Ask for BUY or Bid for SELL after the signal bar closes. A limit uses one confirmed structure level on the valid side, expires after three bars or invalidation, and is not silently converted to market. To reduce complexity, do not continually reprice or immediately replace uncertain/cancelled orders. These rules are a testable starting hypothesis, not evidence that `3/3` has a higher win rate.
- **SL:** place a broker-side initial stop beyond a confirmed opposing invalidation swing, with a tick/spread-aware buffer and correct executable exit side. For a pending entry, the entry pivot must not also be the stop pivot. Round to the broker's tick grid and recheck stops/freeze distances. If a valid level is unavailable, show the reason; do not fabricate a price or widen it without recalculating risk.
- **One main TP:** a candidate is the nearest viable confirmed opposing swing, adjusted to the exit quote side and tick grid. Apply the requested extra `main TP distance > current spread` screen and broker requirements; do not count spread a second time in account-currency P/L. If no viable structure exists, show `HOLD: no valid target`. This is an explicit heuristic, not proof that the target will be reached.
- **Timeframes:** retain shorter-horizon M5 scalping and longer M15/M30 analysis. The existing 4/6/8-bar windows are possible research starting points, not validated deadlines or a guarantee that every M15/M30 TP must be farther in price than every M5 TP. A live time-based forced exit would be a separate policy requiring agreement.
- **Exit policy selected:** one broker-side TP and SL, with no TP1/TP2 partial-close ladder. Stop and target stay attached to the broker position; no time stop, trailing stop or other forced exit is implied by the research horizons. Any later additional exit policy would be a separate, explicitly agreed change.

### D. Sizing, execution and account safety

- Calculate planned account-currency loss with `OrderCalcProfit` using the actual intended direction, executable entry and SL; fail safely if it cannot be calculated [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/trading/ordercalcprofit). Round volume **down** to a valid broker step and recheck the resulting planned risk. If the minimum lot exceeds the user's budget, HOLD rather than silently exceeding it. A gap, spread change, slippage or unknown fee can still make realized loss exceed the budget.
- Check trade permission, quote freshness, symbol trade mode, broker price/volume grid, SL/TP distances and direction-specific margin. Recheck immediately before sending. Attach the initial SL to the order; do not assume a local stop protects against terminal or network failure.
- Treat request accepted, partial fill, completed deal, rejected and uncertain/timeout as different states. Check the server retcode and reconcile orders, deals and positions; never immediately retry an uncertain result and accidentally duplicate an order. On restart, recover broker state before allowing a new entry.
- The user confirmed one active position or pending idea **per timeframe and symbol**; M5, M15 and M30 positions may coexist. Netting accounts have one net position per symbol; magic numbers do not make three independent positions. Retain a clear `hedging required` HOLD for new entries. The later Automate plan proposes showing gross combined exposure without silently imposing a new aggregate cap. Multiple terminals need an explicit coordination policy; a local lock alone is not cross-terminal protection.
- Record actual profit, commission, swap and `DEAL_FEE` when the broker supplies them and they are attributable. No manual commission input is required. An unknown future commission or slippage should be shown as **unknown**, not invented as zero or advertised as net profitability.

### E. Panel and trace

```text
TF   TREND  REGIME  SIGNAL  PLAN/TRADE  ENTRY   SL   TP   REASON
M5   UP     STEADY  BUY     READY       ...     ...  ...  checks passed
M15  UP     MIXED   BUY     HOLD        ...     ...  ...  target <= spread
M30  DOWN   STEADY  HOLD    --          --      --   --   score insufficient
```

This is a mock-up, not an actual market recommendation. Keep signal, proposed price plan and broker-confirmed execution visibly separate. Expose the active checks and at least the first blocking reason, with numerical spread/target/risk details. Log signal time, quotes, regime, votes, planned levels, request and result, fill and all attributable deal costs. Collect outcome data as observation only; do not let a small-sample learner silently adjust trading.

## 5. Old versus proposed

| Area | Current SimpleEA | Proposed candidate, subject to agreement |
|---|---|---|
| Trend/signal | Adaptive Ehlers trend/ROC, structure vote and signal gates. | Similar transparent closed-bar score; no claim that the votes are independent or calibrated. |
| Regime | Hurst label; M5 can borrow M15; ER participates in signal/range rules. | Explainable ER/range context with explicit `unknown` and short hysteresis; compare against Hurst, do not presume improvement. |
| Entry | `3/3` can be market; `2/3` limit; live limits may be refreshed. | Candidate retains market/limit distinction but uses one fixed plan and no hidden conversion or price chasing. |
| Stop | Confirmed swing plus spread/tick buffer; broker stop. | Confirmed swing plus quote-side/tick buffer; broker stop; calculate money risk with `OrderCalcProfit`. |
| Target | Three independent pre-stop excursion quantiles, each possibly swing-capped; main TP3 has spread screen. | One structure-based main TP, no TP1/TP2 partials and no ATR-multiplier TP/SL. |
| Evidence at runtime | Requires at least 30 matching replay observations for a trade plan. | No retrospective replay gate in the initial candidate; collect and validate outcome evidence separately. |
| Accounts | New entries require hedging. | SimpleEA-style per-timeframe ownership needs hedging; a netting alternative requires separate approval. |
| Complexity | Three lanes, replay and staged-exit recovery. | One deterministic signal/plan pipeline, minimal broker-state recovery and clear HOLD reasons; optional features only after validation. |

## 6. Validation the user can perform

The user decides whether and when to test. Before relying on a new EA, the relevant checks are:

1. Compile in **MetaEditor** and run automated boundary tests for BUY/SELL, bid/ask, ticks versus points, minimum/step volume, risk failure, price rounding, stop/freeze rules, request retcodes, partial fills, restart, netting/hedging and missing data.
2. Compare current SimpleEA, a simple baseline and the candidate on the **same broker, symbol, real-tick dates, spread, commission and account settings**. Use real-tick Strategy Tester mode and realistic request delay. Test M5, M15 and M30 separately.
3. Use chronological development/forward windows with parameters frozen before each forward period; avoid overlapping outcome labels leaking across boundaries. Keep a final untouched period, and record every variant tried. More complex purged/embargoed validation can supplement—not replace—a genuinely later holdout [1](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/21603).
4. Report signal counts, directional hit rate at declared horizons, actual fill rate, TP-first/SL-first/still-open outcomes, net expectancy, fees, drawdown, trade count, stability by regime/session and HOLD reasons. A blocked setup's hypothetical outcome is a simulation, not an actual broker fill. Compare regimes and each optional gate by ablation; do not optimize accuracy alone or add filters simply to reduce the trade count.
5. Include wide-spread, slippage, gap and market-closed scenarios, plus a forward demo before any voluntary live deployment. No SL guarantees a maximum realized loss.

**Checks actually run for this review:** SimpleEA's Python/source-extraction suite passed **15/15**; Emtt's archived Python/synthetic suite passed **611/611**. Autotrader's Python lint, score simulation, stop-control tests, trailing-policy tests and volume-profile simulation also passed. These do not compile MQL5, simulate the complete broker lifecycle or prove profitability. No MetaEditor, MT5 Strategy Tester or live broker execution was run.

## 7. Later decisions and next step

The user named the new EA **Automate**, confirmed that all three timeframe positions may coexist on one symbol, and requested automatic broker/account discovery and optional percentage-risk bypass. No broker identity can be inferred from the source. The standalone [Automate plan](../automate.md) records those decisions, proposes a 1%-equity starting point, and replaces the preliminary architecture above. No additional repository is needed for this research.
